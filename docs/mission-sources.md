# Mission and instrument content sources

UI-FLOW-001; checked 2026-11-14. Source research by the archive specialist, implementation in `Home`, `Instrument`, `MissionReferences` and `InstrumentDiagram`. No scientific data or processing changed.

## Evidence and version distinctions

- [Supplied Drive archive](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/0/folders/1RN-2iIkfyLnw28LPaGyB5fvQ0iR9_Kc4) contains the mission paper and historical processing documents.
- [Murtagh et al. (2002), Drive copy](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0BwFfxWNbTZdsMWVEaXJqbFBvM1E/view?resourcekey=0-VLcE5IOU5BIZzXFueN_-SQ), pp.311–313: scientific objectives, spacecraft, limb scans, telescope, receivers, spectrometers and calibration. Drive modified 2015-02-04. [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1139/P01-157).
- [Drive L1 ATBD](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B4UItRdx7Kr_Y1kwRnY2SmRIWTQ/view?resourcekey=0-3uZY1AVI_nAlPELRfV16Mw), v0.1, 2015-11-26, §§1.1–1.2.2 pp.1–4: instrument and data levels. This is older than public `documents/L1_ATBD.pdf`, v1.0, 2020-09-09; public links use the newer file.
- [Drive L2 ATBD](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B4UItRdx7Kr_Nlk2LXdONFV2c3c/view?resourcekey=0-1CfUAcqhsV8Mw002jhLH3g), Patrick Eriksson, v1.0, 2020-03-07, §§1.1–1.2.2: passive limb measurements, calibration and retrieval, receiver configuration; same version date as local public file. Channel spacing and spectral resolution are distinguished. Hardware receiver frequency does not establish which channel produced a retrieved temperature product.
- [Chalmers overview](https://www.chalmers.se/en/research/infrastructure/oso/telescopes-and-instruments/radio-astronomy-facilities/odin/), updated 2026-10-27: international partnership, approximately 250 kg three-axis platform, nominal early 600 km Sun-synchronous orbit, 1.1 m telescope, four tunable receivers over 486–504 and 541–581 GHz plus fixed 118.75 GHz receiver.
- [Swedish National Space Agency mission conclusion](https://en.rymdstyrelsen.se/upptack-rymden/bloggen/2026/08/svenskledda-satelliten-odin-har-avslutat-sitt-uppdrag-efter-25-ar-i-rymden/), published 2026-08-13: launch 2001-02-20, re-entry 2026-08-03. Historical operational descriptions are not current mission status. Mission dates do not imply released product completeness.

## Visual provenance and limits

`public/images/odin665.jpg` is an existing mission illustration, traced to Git commit `3427699`, not a photograph taken in orbit. No local creator/license record was established. The page retains it with a factual illustration caption and makes no new attribution or reuse-license claim. Chalmers credits a different image to SSC; that was not transferred to this asset.

Both block diagrams are original responsive HTML/CSS representations based on the above descriptions, explicitly conceptual and not to scale. The platform diagram summarizes functional support and data flow; the SMR diagram separates the instrument path from ground processing. They are not copied publication figures, electrical schematics or complete hardware inventories. OSIRIS data processing is separate from the SMR products on this site.
